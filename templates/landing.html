Real-time voice agent

A voice that answers as fast as you can speak — in Hindi or English.

Eva listens through your mic, understands either language mid-sentence, thinks out loud, and replies in a natural voice — all in a couple of seconds, no typing required.

01 · Listens

Streaming speech recognition

Your mic audio streams live and is transcribed as you speak, recognizing Hindi and English in the same sentence.

02 · Thinks

Short, spoken-style replies

A language model drafts a concise reply built for the ear, not the eye — a sentence or two, never a wall of text.

03 · Speaks

Natural streaming voice

The reply is spoken back sentence by sentence in a warm, native-sounding voice, matching whichever language you used.
